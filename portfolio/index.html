About

I grew up between Lyon and Rotterdam, studied applied maths in Delft, and ended up in Amsterdam because the bikes are flat and the coffee is decent. I started in classical NLP — POS tagging, CRFs, the whole museum — and gradually drifted toward the kind of large-model engineering that requires both a strong opinion about gradient accumulation and a tolerance for very long YAML files.

I'm most useful in the part of a project that lives between "we have a notebook that works on one CSV" and "this thing serves three million requests a day". I write a lot of eval code, a fair amount of glue, and just enough modelling to keep my hands dirty.

Selected work

Document understanding for a Dutch insurer

2024 · contract · 8 months

Built a pipeline that turns scanned claims into structured JSON. Mix of layout-aware transformers, classical OCR, and a small army of rule-based fallbacks for the cases where the model is wrong in confident ways. Cut manual review time by roughly 60% and, more importantly, made the manual review queue something humans were willing to look at.

  • pydantic
  • fastapi

Recommendation tower retraining

2023 · in-house

Rewrote a two-tower recommender that had drifted into "nobody touches it" territory. The interesting parts were not the model; they were the offline-to-online consistency checks, the feature store migration, and the slow process of convincing stakeholders that NDCG@10 isn't the only metric that matters.

  • pytorch
  • feast
  • airflow

Eval harness for an internal LLM

2023 · open source spin-off

A small framework for running deterministic, replayable evaluations on a moving target model. Captures inputs, outputs, prompts, and seeds so that "the model got worse" is a debuggable claim instead of a hallway complaint.

  • python
  • duckdb
  • jinja

Image-similarity for an archive

2022 · research collab

Worked with a cultural heritage institute on near-duplicate detection across roughly 4M scanned photographs. The model was small; the interesting work was in the curation interface and the threshold tuning that let archivists correct mistakes without losing trust in the system.
